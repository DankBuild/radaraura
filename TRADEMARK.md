# The RadarAura name

"RadarAura" and the radar mark identify the RadarAura air monitor by DankBuild.
The licence ([LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md)) covers the files; this page sums up the
rules for the name.

- **Every build keeps the name.** The name on the front of the case, the radar
  mark with the name on the back, and the RadarAura title on the screen stay on
  every device, also one built only for yourself (licence section 2).
- **Modified versions you share** keep the name and say they are modified:
  "RadarAura, modified by \<your name\>". They must not suggest that DankBuild
  made or approved the changes.
- **Selling** anything under the RadarAura name requires written permission
  from DankBuild.
- The name is not licensed for anything else: other products, services,
  domains or accounts.
