# The RadarAura name

"RadarAura" and the radar mark identify the original air monitor by DankBuild.
The licences ([LICENSE.md](LICENSE.md)) cover the files; this page covers the name.

- **Your own build** may keep the RadarAura name on the case and screen, and you
  are welcome to. It is not required.
- **Modified versions you share** must not be presented as the original or as
  made or approved by DankBuild. Say "based on RadarAura by DankBuild" and link
  to https://radaraura.com.
- **Selling** anything under the RadarAura name requires written permission from
  DankBuild, even when a commercial licence for the files has been granted.
- The name is not licensed for anything else: other products, services,
  domains or accounts.
