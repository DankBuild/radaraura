# Case

Printed parts, generated by `atmo4.py`. The STLs are already laid flat in print
orientation. Outside size about 72 x 149 x 42 mm. The SEN66 sits in its own
chamber below the screen with its air ports against the front openings.

## Choose how to print

The RadarAura name on the front and the radar mark on the back are part of the
case in both options (see [TRADEMARK.md](../TRADEMARK.md)).

### Option A: one colour (any printer)

Print these three parts. The name and the radar mark come out recessed 0.6 mm.

| File | Part | Lay it |
|---|---|---|
| `stl/v4_front_body.stl` | Front shell: screen window, sensor chamber in the chin, air openings, recessed name | screen face down |
| `stl/v4_rear_cover.stl` | Rear cover: screw posts, snap hooks, USB window, recessed radar mark | back down |
| `stl/v4_usb_insert.stl` | USB-C insert that clicks into the cover's window | floor down |

The recessed name is subtle in one colour. To make it readable, rub a little
acrylic paint (or a paint-marker line) into the letters and wipe the surface
clean before it dries.

### Option B: two colours (name and mark in a second colour)

The same three parts, plus two inlays that fill the recesses flush:

| File | Part |
|---|---|
| `stl/v4_front_body_name.stl` | The name, fills the recess in the front shell |
| `stl/v4_rear_cover_icon.stl` | The radar mark and name, fills the recess in the rear cover |

**Any printer:** everything above is in `stl/`, and STL files work in every
slicer (PrusaSlicer, Cura, OrcaSlicer, Bambu Studio and others).

**Multi-material printer (AMS, MMU, tool changer), from the STLs:**

1. Load the body and its inlay as **one object with two parts**:
   `v4_front_body.stl` + `v4_front_body_name.stl` for the front,
   `v4_rear_cover.stl` + `v4_rear_cover_icon.stl` for the back.
   - Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer, PrusaSlicer: select both files in one import and
     answer **Yes** to "load as a single object with multiple parts".
   - Cura: load both, select both and use **Merge Models** (Ctrl+Alt+G).
2. Give the inlay part your second filament (extruder).
3. Slice. The colour only changes during the first three layers.

Bambu Studio / OrcaSlicer tip for a clean edge around the letters: under
Quality, set **Only one wall on first layer** on, **Wall generator** Arachne
and **Infill/wall overlap** 30%. With the defaults the slicer traces 2 walls
around every letter (a glossy outline) and leaves tiny pinholes where the fill
meets them.

**Single-nozzle printer (manual filament swap):** load only the body STL,
slice, and add a filament change at the layer above 0.6 mm (layer 4):
"Pause" in Bambu Studio and OrcaSlicer, "Add color change" (M600) in
PrusaSlicer, the "Pause at height" post-processing script in Cura. At the
pause, load the second colour, let it purge clean, print that one layer, then
switch back. The name shows in the second colour at the bottom of the recess,
plus a thin line around the edge.

## Optional: screws

The case plugs tightly together: the cover clicks in with snap hooks and holds
without screws. For extra security, 4 M2 pan-head screws go through the cover
into the board's standoffs. If you want them, print `stl/v4_screw_coupon.stl`
(one screw post) first to test the M2 fit against your board.

## Printing and assembly

- PETG, 0.2 mm layers, no supports.
- Press the cover in until the snap hooks click; it holds tightly on its own.
- Optional screws: 4 M2 pan-head, length depending on how deep your standoffs
  are threaded. The heads sink about 2 mm into recesses in the cover: if a
  screw stops before its head is down in the recess, it is bottoming out in the
  standoff, so use a shorter one (or cut it down).
- Push the USB insert into the cover's window from the outside until it clicks.

The full build, including the sensor wiring, is at
<https://radaraura.com/build/>.

## Optional: desk stand

`stl/stand_15deg.stl` and `stl/stand_25deg.stl` tilt the case back 15 or 25
degrees. The case drops in from above; the stand holds it by its sides, so the
air openings and the USB cable stay free. Prints flat on its base, no supports.
For another angle: `.venv/bin/python stand.py 20` writes `stl/stand_20deg.stl`.

## Changing the design

Every dimension you might tune is in the `PARAMS` block at the top of `atmo4.py`.

```sh
python3 -m venv .venv
.venv/bin/pip install trimesh manifold3d shapely numpy
.venv/bin/python atmo4.py      # writes stl/
```

The script prints the size, watertightness and body count of each part. Both
should report `watertight=True` and `bodies=1`.
